{% elif page == 'faq' %}
How do I order a car?

Create an account, pick a car from Cars, customise it, enter delivery details and pay by UPI QR.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. An account lets you see and track all your orders in My Orders.

How do I track my order?

Log in and open My Orders to see the live status of every order.

What do the order statuses mean?

Pending (waiting for confirmation) → Confirmed → Production → Shipped → Delivered.

Is GST included?

Yes. The total shown on the order page includes 28% GST.
